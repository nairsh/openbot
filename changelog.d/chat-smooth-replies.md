### Changed

- A reply shows complete when it arrives, as in a phone's messages. It does not type itself out.
- The message box corrects a misspelled word with the macOS spellchecker when you type a space or a
  punctuation mark.
- The "Working" line shows after your sent message settles, and folds away when the reply arrives.
- The message box focus ring is thinner.

### Fixed

- The steps list under "Worked for" folds away smoothly when you close it.
- A chat fades in when you switch agents, so the change is not abrupt.
